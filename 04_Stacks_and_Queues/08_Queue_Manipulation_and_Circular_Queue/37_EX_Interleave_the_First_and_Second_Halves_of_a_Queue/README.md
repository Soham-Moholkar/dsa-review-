## What you are meant to learn

Interleave two contiguous halves while preserving each half's internal order.

## Exercise contract

Input length is even. Mutate the given queue; leftmost is front.

## Concepts required

- queue rotation
- stable interleaving

## Prerequisites

- reverse first K
- auxiliary queue
