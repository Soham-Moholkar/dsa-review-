## What you are meant to learn

Keep the original order when removing the middle entry from LIFO storage.

## Exercise contract

For even n, remove the lower middle (index (n-1)/2 from the bottom). Mutate the stack; rightmost is top.

## Concepts required

- recursion
- position
- restoration

## Prerequisites

- call stack
- stack size
